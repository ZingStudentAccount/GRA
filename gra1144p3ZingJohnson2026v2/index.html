Synopsis

Some memories are worth fighting to keep.

Joel Barish is stunned to learn that his ex-girlfriend Clementine has paid a small company, Lacuna, to erase every memory of their relationship. Heartbroken, he signs up for the same procedure.

But as his memories of Clementine disappear one by one, Joel realizes he still loves her. Trapped inside his own mind, he races to hide her somewhere the erasers can't reach.
